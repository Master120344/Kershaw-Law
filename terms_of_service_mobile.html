Website Terms

Last updated: October 5, 2026

These website notices explain the purpose of kershawlaw.com and the limits of its general information. Please contact the firm to discuss advice specific to your business or a potential engagement.

1. General information

This website provides general information about The Kershaw Law Firm P.C. and the H-2A and H-2B temporary worker programs. Website content is not legal advice and should not be used as a substitute for advice about your particular circumstances.

Immigration requirements, government fees, filing procedures, and agency guidance can change. Confirm current requirements with the relevant agency and seek legal advice before making decisions or relying on a filing deadline.

2. Contacting the firm

Reading this website or contacting the firm does not, by itself, establish an attorney-client relationship. A request for information or a consultation is a request to discuss the possibility of representation. The firm must determine whether it can accept your matter and agree on the scope and terms of any engagement.

Please provide only a general description in an initial inquiry. Avoid sending confidential documents or sensitive personal information before discussing an appropriate way to provide them. Information relating to prospective clients may be subject to professional obligations even when representation does not follow.

3. Legal outcomes

Website descriptions do not promise visa approval, a particular processing time, worker availability, or any other legal result. Agency decisions and case outcomes depend on the applicable requirements and the facts of each matter.

4. Third-party resources

This website may link to government guidance, maps, reviews, and other resources maintained by third parties. Their content, services, and terms are provided by those operators. A link does not replace an assessment of how that information applies to your circumstances.

5. Information and privacy

Our Privacy Policy describes the information requested by website forms and the website tools used to support inquiries and measure performance. Contact the firm with questions about the handling of information associated with your inquiry or matter.

6. Updates and questions

The date above identifies the most recent revision to these notices. If you find an error, encounter a website issue, or have a question about its content, please contact the firm.
