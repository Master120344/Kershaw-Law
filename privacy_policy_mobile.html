Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This page explains the information requested through kershawlaw.com and the website tools used to support inquiries and measure site performance. For questions about information associated with an existing client matter, please contact the firm.

1. Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, selected service, and message. A phone number is optional. The consultation request form also asks for your company name, phone number, position or job title, approximate number of workers, visa program, job duties, additional details, and referral source.

Please provide a general description of your business needs. Avoid including passport numbers, Social Security numbers, medical records, payment details, or confidential documents in an initial website inquiry. If representation is discussed, ask the firm which additional information is needed and how to provide it.

2. Inquiries and intake

Information submitted through the website is provided to the firm so it can respond to your inquiry and discuss the possibility of representation. Additional information may be needed to assess your request, check for conflicts, or prepare for a consultation.

Contacting the firm does not, by itself, establish an attorney-client relationship. The handling of information relating to a prospective or existing client is subject to applicable law and professional obligations.

3. Website tools and external services

The inquiry forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish legitimate submissions from automated activity. Some pages also load a Cloudflare analytics beacon that measures website performance. These services can process technical information associated with your visit.

For information about Cloudflare's handling of information, see its Privacy Policy. Cloudflare also describes its Web Analytics data collection.

The site hosts its fonts locally. Loading external tools, including those described above, involves a request to the provider. Browser controls can limit some website storage or requests, although blocking resources may affect the website or its forms.

4. Records and retention

Handling and retention requirements can differ between a website inquiry and an active client matter. They may depend on the information involved, the purpose of the communication, and applicable legal or professional obligations. Contact the firm to ask about the handling, recipients, or retention of information related to your inquiry or matter.

5. Privacy requests

You may contact the firm to ask about information you have provided or to request a correction. Access, deletion, and other rights may be available depending on applicable law and the circumstances. A request may be subject to identity verification, recordkeeping requirements, and legal or professional obligations.

Please describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive information. Contact the firm if you need an appropriate way to provide supporting documents.

6. Links to other websites

Links to government resources, maps, reviews, and other websites take you to services operated by others. Review the applicable privacy information on those sites before providing information to them.

7. Updates to this page

The date above identifies the most recent revision to this page. Check this page for updates, and contact the firm with questions about current information handling.
