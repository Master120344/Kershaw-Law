The Kershaw Law Firm P.C. is based in Austin, Texas. Our employer immigration work focuses on the H-2A agricultural and H-2B nonagricultural programs.

The first question is whether a program fits your circumstances. The work involved, the reason for a temporary need, the proposed dates, and the employer's responsibilities all matter. A labor shortage alone does not establish eligibility.

We approach these matters through case assessment, filing preparation, and attention to the obligations that continue after approval. The services provided in a particular matter are defined by the engagement agreement.
