Two distinct programs

The right starting point

H-2A and H-2B are employer-driven programs with different eligibility standards and operating requirements. Neither is a general solution for every unfilled job.

The Kershaw Law Firm P.C. assists employers with evaluating program fit, preparing filings, and understanding applicable requirements. Representation, fees, and any additional services are established in a written engagement agreement.

01 / Agricultural employment

H-2A

For agricultural labor or services of a temporary or seasonal nature.

The H-2A process generally begins with a job order and a temporary labor certification application to the U.S. Department of Labor. The employer must meet recruitment requirements and demonstrate that sufficient qualified U.S. workers are not available, and that hiring foreign workers will not adversely affect similarly employed U.S. workers.

Case planning
Review the agricultural work, proposed employment dates, work locations, and supporting records.
Labor certification
Prepare the job order and certification application, and advise on recruitment and the required employment terms.
Immigration petition
Prepare the USCIS petition following labor certification and address agency requests within the agreed scope of representation.
Employer obligations
Review applicable wages, housing, transportation, worker protections, and recordkeeping requirements.
Department of Labor: H-2A program

02 / Nonagricultural employment

H-2B

For nonagricultural jobs supported by a qualifying temporary need.

For H-2B, the employer must establish a one-time occurrence, seasonal need, peakload need, or intermittent need. The temporary nature of the employer's need matters even when the underlying job is permanent.

Temporary need
Evaluate the business's employment history and records supporting the requested period of need.
Wage and recruitment steps
Prepare the prevailing wage request, job order, and labor certification application, and advise on U.S. worker recruitment.
Petition planning
Prepare the USCIS petition and consider filing requirements, current visa availability, and any applicable cap rules.
Ongoing requirements
Review applicable wage, transportation, recruitment, worker protection, and recordkeeping obligations.
Department of Labor: H-2B program

Before you file

Plan beyond
the application.

Recruitment, wages, job terms, and worker protections are part of program participation. Obligations may also apply to U.S. workers in corresponding employment. These requirements deserve attention before a filing is made.

Agency processing, visa issuance, and admission are separate stages. No law firm can guarantee approval, worker arrival, or a particular completion date.
